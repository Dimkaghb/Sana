What the phone can measure

Touch and motion
Compare a tap with the phone’s acceleration around that instant.
Rhythm and trajectory
Measure keypad timing and the path of the confirmation slider.

Recipient digits stay out of telemetry. A denied sensor permission leaves a gap in the evidence.

How a transfer is scored

  1. Phone → FastAPI · Send a bounded telemetry session over HTTPS.
  2. Physics and identity · Inspect tap recoil and cadence. Compare a 128-dimensional embedding with prior sessions.
  3. Policy → SQLite · Persist the decision, return the mobile receipt, and update the analyst over WebSocket.

One operator, multiple accounts

Owner verification compares a session with an explicitly enrolled account baseline.

Cross-account search checks whether similar sessions recur under different account IDs. The analyst can inspect which accounts contributed to a ring alert.

What a customer sees

Success
The mobile app returns a simulated receipt.
Step-up
Additional verification is required when evidence is incomplete or risk is elevated.
Mirage hold

A pilot for BCC transfers

Begin with a consented mobile-transfer pilot. Measure false holds, missed handoffs and response time across phones before considering JuniorBank or business accounts.
